About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Scholar in the Cancer Biology Division at the Perelman School of Medicine, University of Pennsylvania. My research focuses on understanding the role of E3 ubiquitin ligases in cell cycle regulation, DNA damage response, viral infection, and cancer. I work on the development of targeted protein degradation strategies, including PROTACs, to design innovative therapeutic approaches. My long-term goal is to establish an independent research program in neuroscience and cancer biology.
